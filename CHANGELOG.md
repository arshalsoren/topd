## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-17

First public version.

### Added
- Six metric cards: CPU, Memory, GPU, Storage, System, Network, each with its own chart.
- Top 5 process lists by CPU and by memory, with search and sort.
- Menu bar at-a-glance values for one to three cards, and a choice of menu bar icon.
- Twelve themes, including System, which follows macOS light and dark.
- Reorderable cards, and a popover that sizes itself to the cards that are on.
- Launch at login from Preferences.
- `run.sh` builds and starts the app bundle locally.
